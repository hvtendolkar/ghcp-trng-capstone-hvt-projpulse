### Adoption Risks & Mitigation
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| **Team confusion about principles** | Comprehensive guides + FAQ + role-specific training |
| **Slow PR reviews due to checklists** | Checklists will become faster with practice (week 2-3) |
| **Too many violations initially** | Expected in learning phase; violations decrease week 2-3 |
